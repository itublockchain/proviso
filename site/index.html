How it works

Say it once. Proviso shops by your rules.

You set the limits in plain words. The agent does the watching and the buying, and a contract checks every payment against what you wrote.

01

Describe it

Chat with Proviso like you would with a friend who knows prices.

“Sony 55-inch TV within a month, never above $500, buy it yourself under $400.”

02

It watches, then buys

Proviso compares live prices across stores and waits for the right moment. Under your auto limit it buys on its own, only from verified merchants.

03

Above that, it asks you

A fresh World ID approval for that exact order. Above your max, it simply can't pay: the contract reverts, whatever the agent was told.

Why it's safe

The agent can shop. It can't break your rules.

Most agent payments hand an AI spending power and hope it behaves. Proviso moves the guarantees on-chain: the agent's key can only ask the contract to pay, and the contract checks your rules first.

Rules on ENSv2

Each request lives at [item].[category].[you].proviso.eth. The contract reads its limits on-chain at every purchase. Your wallet owns the names; the AI can only write a status.

Human approval with World ID

World ID for Agents, bound to the exact order and to your own World ID. Approvals expire in 5 minutes. A different person, or a stale approval, buys nothing.

Your own wallet

One signature to set up. No deposit into an agent wallet. Monthly budgets per category that reset on their own.
